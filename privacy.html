Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

In short: SnapPlanet collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you make in the app stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What stays on your device

Pictures you make, searches, events and forecasts already loaded, your own views and your settings are kept on your iPhone or iPad, so the app works without a network. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud

Saved snaps and followed places are kept in your private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices. They are stored by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer of SnapPlanet cannot see or read them.

Location

Only when you tap “Frame where I am”, the app reads your position once to move the map there. The position is not stored and is never sent anywhere.

Photos

When you save a picture, a video or a wallpaper to Photos, SnapPlanet adds it to an album named SnapPlanet. It does not read or send your other photos.

Notifications and event alerts

Pass reminders and change notices are scheduled on your device. If you turn on event alerts, your device saves subscriptions in Apple’s iCloud (the kinds of event you chose, and the location of followed places, to match events within 100 km), under an anonymous identifier Apple gives the app; Apple uses them to deliver the alerts. The developer cannot list them or link them to you. Turning alerts off removes them.

Services the app reads

To show pictures and events, the app downloads public data from: AWS Earth Search (Element 84, Sentinel-2 images), Microsoft Planetary Computer (Landsat, MODIS and GOES images), the published place and event pictures on GitHub Pages, USGS, GDACS, NASA EONET and the Copernicus Emergency Management Service, and Apple Maps. These requests carry the area being looked at, never an identifier or anything about you; like any website, these services see your device’s network address. Their own privacy policies apply to their servers.

Translation

Event descriptions are translated by Apple’s translation on your device.

Purchases

SnapPlanet Pro is bought and managed through Apple. The developer receives no payment details and no personal information.

Children

SnapPlanet collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the app ever collects data, this page will say so before that version is released, with the date above changed.

Contact

Write to snapplanetsupport@gmail.com. Answers in English or French.
