Help

A picture is mostly cloud

Satellites see the weather of the day they passed. Choose another date in the calendar: each day shows its cloud and whether it covers the whole frame. With Pro, “Remove clouds” replaces clouds with clear ground from other dates (up to half the frame).

Part of the picture is grey stripes

The satellite did not cover the whole frame that day. “Fill tiles” completes it from the nearest dates.

The newest pictures are missing

Sentinel-2 images arrive within a day; Landsat about two weeks after it passed. A followed place notifies you when a new picture arrives.

How far back can I go?

The last two months for free. With Pro: every year since 1984 (Landsat), 2015 (Sentinel-2) or 2001 (MODIS), and every past event.

Pictures are slow, or use mobile data

A first quick preview comes at once; on Wi-Fi the sharp picture follows. On mobile data the app only sharpens when you tap “Sharpen”, and says how much it will download (a 12 km picture is about 18 MB the first time, much less afterwards).

My snaps are not on my other devices

They sync through your private iCloud: check that you are signed in to iCloud on each device, with iCloud Drive on.

I don’t receive event alerts

In Following, check that alerts are on and that SnapPlanet may send notifications (iPhone Settings → Notifications). Alerts need iCloud.

Restore a purchase

Settings (the gear at the top right) → Get SnapPlanet Pro → Restore purchases. Pro bought on one Apple Account works on all its devices.

Cancel a subscription

In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the App Store. Pro stays until the end of the period paid.

Delete my data

Delete a snap from its page, or the app to remove everything on the device. Synced snaps can be removed in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → SnapPlanet.
